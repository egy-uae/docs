1. Portal Overview

Al Massriya Al Emaratiya operates a multi-market digital B2B trade hub linking Egypt and the United Arab Emirates. Our platform integrates automated RFQ desks, live logistics tracking, secure commercial agency channels, and specialized divisions.

2. Trade Divisions Structure

The platform houses specialized corridors catering to industrial, commercial, and hospitality needs:

  • Food Division: Managing safe foodstuff pathways from Cairo to Sharjah.
  • Hotel Supplies: Direct procurement pipelines from Port Said to Jebel Ali.
  • Chemicals & Cosmetics: Regulated shipping routes from Alexandria to Khalifa Port.
  • OS&E Sourcing & Furniture: Comprehensive supply chains for office, banquet, and hotel setups.

3. Logistics & Supply Chain

We provide end-to-end multi-modal transport solutions incorporating ocean freight, air cargo, customs clearance, and secure warehousing across key logistics hubs in both countries.

4. Digital Systems & Marketplace

The core infrastructure runs on a modern Tech OS architecture supporting real-time inventory checks, secure API requests, and direct integration with our global marketplace portal at amae.egy-uae.com.
